Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer in-person tutoring?

No. I offer one-on-one online ASVAB tutoring.

How do we meet?

Lessons are conducted online using Google Meet. You only need a computer or laptop and a standard web browser.

Do I choose my lesson times when I sign up?

No. I schedule lessons personally with each student. This allows me to maintain flexibility and find a schedule that works for both of us.

How do I pay?

I accept Venmo, Cash App, and Zelle. After reviewing your intake information, I'll provide payment instructions.

Which package should I choose?

If you're unsure, the $50 Diagnostic & Consultation is a good place to start. If you have a specific test date approaching, the $685 Intensive Course may be appropriate. The four-week course provides additional time for preparation and review.

What if my schedule is unusual?

That's okay. Rather than requiring you to choose from a fixed online calendar, I coordinate lesson times directly with you.

Meet Your Tutor

Hi! My name is Hanees. I'm an educator with degrees and professional experience in engineering and law, and I've been tutoring students for more than 25 years.

I began tutoring in college and discovered a talent for breaking down difficult concepts into simpler ideas and helping students build confidence.

My own academic experience strongly shaped my approach. I went from struggling significantly in math to becoming the highest scorer in my class. That experience taught me the value of clear explanations, structured practice, and having someone identify exactly where you're getting stuck.

I later studied engineering at Loughborough University in the UK before earning degrees in computer engineering, electrical and computer engineering, and law at the University of Arizona.

Professionally, I've worked as a software engineer across the aerospace, semiconductor, travel, and healthcare industries and am also a licensed attorney.

Today, tutoring is my full-time passion. My goal is to help students understand the material, develop effective test-taking strategies, and approach the ASVAB feeling prepared and confident.
