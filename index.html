Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer in-person tutoring?

No. I offer one-on-one online ASVAB tutoring.

How do we meet?

Lessons are conducted online using Google Meet. You only need a computer or laptop and a standard web browser.

How do I schedule my lessons?

You don't have to select a time through an automated calendar. Email or text me with your package, target test date, and availability. I personally coordinate your lesson schedule.

When should I start?

Ideally, start early enough to allow time for practice and improvement. However, the intensive two-week course is specifically designed for students working with a shorter preparation timeline.

Can I start with the $50 diagnostic?

Yes. The diagnostic and consultation is an excellent way to determine your current level and decide which preparation approach makes the most sense for you.

Do I have to use Stripe or Calendly?

No. Payment and scheduling are handled directly with me through Venmo, Cash App, or Zelle, and I personally arrange lesson times.

Meet Your Tutor

Hi! My name is Hanees. I'm an educator with degrees and professional experience in engineering and law, and I've been tutoring students for more than 25 years.

I began tutoring in college and discovered that I had a talent for breaking down difficult concepts into simpler ideas and helping students build confidence.

My own academic experience shaped my approach. I went from struggling significantly in math to becoming the highest scorer in my class. That experience taught me the value of clear explanations, structured practice, and having someone who can identify exactly where you're getting stuck.

I later studied engineering at Loughborough University in the UK before earning degrees in computer engineering, electrical and computer engineering, and law at the University of Arizona.

Professionally, I've worked as a software engineer in aerospace, semiconductor, travel, and healthcare industries, and I'm also a licensed attorney.

Today, tutoring is my full-time passion. My goal is simple: help you understand the material, develop effective test-taking strategies, and walk into the ASVAB feeling prepared and confident.
