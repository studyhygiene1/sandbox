A Systematic Approach to ASVAB Preparation

Hi! My name is Hanees. I'm an educator with a background in engineering and law, and I've been tutoring students for more than 25 years.

I believe effective test preparation starts with understanding exactly where a student is starting. From there, we can identify the concepts that need attention, build the necessary skills, and develop strategies for approaching the test with greater confidence.

My own academic experience taught me the value of focused instruction. I went from struggling in math early in my education to becoming the highest scorer in my class and ultimately pursuing engineering, law, and a career in technology.

As a tutor, I focus on breaking difficult concepts into manageable pieces, explaining not only what to do but why it works, and helping students become increasingly independent and confident.

ASVAB preparation is now the sole focus of this tutoring program, allowing the instruction and course structure to be centered specifically around students preparing for the exam.
