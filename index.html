About Study Hygiene

Holistic tutoring that builds lasting skills.

We’re educators at heart, with degrees in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, and Law. We love this work because it gives us the opportunity to make a lasting difference in students’ lives.

Our philosophy is holistic: content mastery, test-taking strategy, and learning psychology. The best results come from building strong, “hygienic” study habits and a clear plan you can actually follow.

For test prep, we use diagnostics to identify gaps before moving into timed drills and high-impact strategy. We help students use tools (like Desmos) effectively and approach exams with calm confidence.
