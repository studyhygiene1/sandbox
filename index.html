About Me

Anxious about your SAT, ACT, ASVAB, or GRE? Struggling with math or writing? Need to prepare for an interview or write a personal statement that helps you stand out? I can help.

I'm an educator with degrees and professional experience in engineering and law.

Tutoring is my full-time passion. Motivated in part by my own early struggles in math, I’m dedicated to helping students overcome obstacles and build confidence. Focused tutoring helped me transform from a D− student into the highest scorer in my class, earning a perfect score on my math final. That experience shaped the trajectory of my academic and professional career.

I graduated high school as valedictorian and the top scorer in my state. I later studied engineering at Loughborough University before earning a full scholarship to the University of Arizona, where I completed my B.Sc. in Computer Engineering, M.Sc. in Electrical & Computer Engineering, and J.D.

Professionally, I have worked as a software engineer across aerospace, semiconductor, travel, and healthcare industries and am also a licensed attorney.

I began tutoring in college and discovered a passion for helping students connect with difficult concepts through simple explanations and meaningful engagement.

Today, with 25+ years of teaching experience, I specialize in helping students exceed their own expectations through structured, individualized instruction.
