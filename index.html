A Systematic Approach to ASVAB Preparation

Hi! My name is Hanees. I'm an educator with a background in engineering and law, and I've been tutoring students for more than 25 years.

I believe effective test preparation starts with understanding exactly where a student is starting. From there, we can identify the concepts that need attention, build the necessary skills, and develop strategies for approaching the test with greater confidence.

My own academic experience taught me the value of focused instruction. I went from struggling in math early in my education to becoming the highest scorer in my class and ultimately pursuing engineering, law, and a career in technology.

As a tutor, I focus on breaking difficult concepts into manageable pieces, explaining not only what to do but why it works, and helping students become increasingly independent and confident.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

Are these ASVAB programs one-on-one?

Yes. All tutoring is private, one-on-one online instruction tailored to the student's needs and goals.

Where are lessons held?

Lessons are conducted online using Google Meet. You only need a computer or laptop and a modern web browser.

Which preparation option should I choose?

If you're unsure, start with a free 15-minute consultation. If you want a detailed assessment of your current strengths, weaknesses, and recommended study strategy, the ASVAB Diagnostic & Strategy Session is currently $40, regularly $80.

How much preparation time do you recommend?

Whenever possible, I recommend allowing approximately 4–8 weeks for preparation. This gives students time to build skills, address weaknesses, practice consistently, and develop confidence before testing.

What if my ASVAB test date is strict and cannot be moved?

Accelerated options are available for students with non-negotiable testing timelines: a 7-Day ASVAB Crash Course with 7 private lessons for $875 and a 2-Week ASVAB Intensive with 12 private lessons for $1,380.

How does weekly preparation work?

Weekly ASVAB Preparation includes 4 private lessons per week for $420. Enrollment is billed week-to-week with no long-term contract, allowing the preparation timeline to be adjusted based on your needs and progress.

Do I need to know my target score before starting?

No. We can discuss your goals, current situation, and testing timeline during a free consultation or explore them in greater depth during the diagnostic session.

How do I get started?

Click any of the Get Started or package buttons on this page. A short intake form will open in a popup. Once I receive your information, I'll review it and contact you directly to discuss the next steps, payment, and scheduling.

How do I pay?

Payment is handled directly rather than through an automated checkout system. Available payment methods include Venmo, Cash App, and Zelle. Payment instructions will be provided when we arrange your tutoring.
