How Prep Works

Your plan depends on your timeline, baseline score, and target score. Here’s the process we use with most students:

1) Diagnostic + Goal Setting

  • Identify strongest sections and the biggest score opportunities
  • Set a realistic score goal and timeline
  • Build a weekly study plan (what to do between sessions)

2) High-Frequency Skill Training

  • Targeted drills for the most-tested topics
  • Clear explanations and repeatable methods
  • Make “hard questions” predictable

3) Strategy + Pacing

  • Timing systems that reduce rushed mistakes
  • When to skip, return, and guess strategically
  • Calculator/Desmos efficiency and checks

4) Practice Tests + Review

  • Score improvement comes from review—not just taking tests
  • We build an error log and fix patterns
  • Turn mistakes into points
