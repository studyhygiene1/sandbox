Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

Are these ASVAB programs one-on-one?

Yes. All tutoring is private, one-on-one online instruction tailored to the student's needs and goals.

When and where are lessons held?

Lessons are conducted online using Google Meet and scheduled according to the student's needs and convenience. We provide services 12 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Which preparation option should I choose?

If you're unsure, start with a free 15-minute consultation. If you want a detailed assessment, the ASVAB Diagnostic & Strategy Session is $40.

How much preparation time do you recommend?

Each student's needs are different. A free consult or a full evaluation ($40) can help you choose the right package. Generally, we recommend allowing approximately 4–8 weeks for preparation. If you wish to proceed with a package without a consult or full evaluation, we recommend the Weekly Preparation option. This gives you the best value, and in our experience, produces the highest scores.

What if my ASVAB test date is strict and cannot be moved?

Accelerated options are available, including a 7-Day ASVAB Crash Course for $735 and a 2-Week ASVAB Intensive for $1,140.

How does weekly preparation work?

Weekly ASVAB Preparation includes 4 private 1-hour lessons per week for $360, saving compared with the individual lesson rate.

Do I need to know my target score before starting?

No. We can discuss your goals and testing timeline during a free consultation or diagnostic session.
